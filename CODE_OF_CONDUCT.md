# Code of Conduct

Bursar is built in public, and the people who use it, report problems in it and contribute to it
come from many places and backgrounds. This code sets out how we expect everyone to treat each
other in Bursar spaces: every bursar-world repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests,
and any channel we run or take part in as Bursar.

## What we expect

- Be considerate. Assume the other person is acting in good faith and has context you may not.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Explain your reasoning and be open to changing your mind.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Give credit for other people's work and ideas.
- Keep other people's private information private.
- When you get something wrong, acknowledge it and put it right.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, intimidation or threats, in public or in private messages.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, body or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery directed at a person, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's personal details, keys or wallet links without their consent.
- Scams, impersonation of Bursar or its maintainers, and promotion of tokens or schemes in project
  spaces.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or continuing an interaction after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Email **[conduct@bursar.world](mailto:conduct@bursar.world)**. Tell us what happened, where and
when, who was involved, and include links or screenshots if you have them. You can report
something that happened to someone else.

Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. If a maintainer is involved in what
you report, they take no part in handling it. We will acknowledge your report within 3 business
days.

## What we may do

After hearing from the people involved, the maintainers decide what is proportionate. That can be
a private note, a public request to change behaviour, removal of comments or content, a temporary
ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban. For serious harm, such as threats or harassment, we
may act at once without warning. We will tell the person reported what we decided and why, unless
doing so would put someone at risk.

If you think a decision about you was wrong, reply to the email that told you about it. A
maintainer who was not part of the original decision will review it.

## Scope

This code applies in all Bursar spaces, and outside them when someone is representing Bursar or
when their conduct elsewhere affects the safety of people in our spaces.
